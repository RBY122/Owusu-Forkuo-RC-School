About Our School

Owusu Forkuo R/C Primary & JHS School proudly serves the Juaben‑Ashanti community with a rich tradition of Catholic education. For decades, our institution has been a cornerstone of faith‑based learning, shaping generations of students with discipline, respect, and knowledge.

Our History

Founded to provide quality Catholic education in Juaben‑Ashanti, the school has grown into a trusted center of learning. Our heritage is rooted in service, moral guidance, and academic excellence, reflecting the values of the Catholic Education Unit of Ghana.

Our Goal

We aim to nurture the whole child — intellectually, spiritually, and socially. By blending modern teaching methods with Ghanaian cultural values, we ensure every learner develops confidence, curiosity, and compassion.

Our Vision

Our vision is to prepare students to become responsible citizens of Ghana and the wider world. We strive to create a supportive environment where faith and learning come together, building a strong foundation for lifelong success.

Faith First

Daily prayer, scripture reflection, and service build our students' moral foundation.

Academic Excellence

Rigorous lessons in literacy, numeracy, science, and Ghanaian studies help students thrive.

Community Spirit

Partnership with families and parish leaders creates a strong, supportive school community.
